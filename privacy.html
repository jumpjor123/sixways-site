Privacy Policy

Last updated: 20 November 2026

Sixways is a number-path puzzle game. It was designed to need as little of your information as possible: there are no Sixways accounts, no advertising, no analytics, and no tracking.

What stays on your device

The app stores the following only on your device, in the app's own storage:

  • your preferences (for example, whether "Show Mistakes" is on, and whether you've seen the in-app tips);
  • your personal best score for each board size and difficulty;
  • your lifetime solve counts and which achievements you have earned, so they can be shown in the app and delivered to Game Center.

This information is never sent to the developer. Deleting the app deletes it. (It may also be included in your own device backups, which you control through iOS.)

Game Center (optional, provided by Apple)

If you are signed in to Game Center, the app submits your winning scores to the Sixways leaderboards and reports achievement progress, so you can compare with friends and other players. This information is handled by Apple under Apple's privacy policy and your Game Center settings, and your Game Center nickname and scores may be visible to other players as you have configured there. If you are not signed in to Game Center, nothing is submitted. The developer does not receive your Apple ID or password and cannot see individual Game Center accounts beyond what Apple displays publicly in leaderboards.

Purchases (provided by Apple)

The optional one-time unlock is sold through the App Store. Apple processes the payment; the developer never receives your payment-card details. Purchase and restore requests are verified on your device using Apple's StoreKit receipts.

What the developer collects

Nothing. The developer operates no server for this app, collects no personal data from it, and does not use any third-party analytics or advertising SDKs.

Children

The app contains no chat, no user-generated content sharing, and no external links except to this policy and the support page.

Changes

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted at this address with a new "last updated" date.
