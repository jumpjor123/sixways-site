Privacy Policy

Last updated: 23 November 2026

Sixways is a number-path puzzle game. It was designed to need as little of your information as possible: there are no Sixways accounts, no advertising, no analytics, and no tracking.

What stays on your device

The app stores the following only on your device, in the app's own storage:

  • your preferences (for example, whether "Show Mistakes" is on, and whether you've seen the in-app tips);
  • your personal best score and best time for each board size and difficulty;
  • your lifetime solve counts and which achievements you have earned, so they can be shown in the app and delivered to the platform game service (Game Center on Apple devices, Play Games on Android).

This information is never sent to the developer. Deleting the app deletes it. (It may also be included in your own device backups, which you control through your device's settings.)

Game services (optional)

On iPhone and iPad the game service is Game Center, provided by Apple; on Android it is Play Games, provided by Google. If you are signed in to the game service, the app submits your winning scores to the Sixways leaderboards and reports achievement progress, so you can compare with friends and other players. This information is handled by Apple or Google under their own privacy policy and your settings there, and your nickname and scores may be visible to other players as you have configured there. If you are not signed in, nothing is submitted. The developer does not receive your account password and cannot see individual game-service accounts beyond what the service displays publicly in leaderboards.

Purchases (provided by the store)

The optional one-time unlock is sold through the App Store on Apple devices and through Google Play on Android. The store processes the payment; the developer never receives your payment-card details. Purchase and restore requests are verified on your device using the store's receipts.

What the developer collects

Nothing. The developer operates no server for this app, collects no personal data from it, and does not use any third-party analytics or advertising SDKs.

Children

The app contains no chat, no user-generated content sharing, and no external links except to this policy and the support page.

Changes

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted at this address with a new "last updated" date.

Contact

Questions about this policy: privacy@sixways.app
