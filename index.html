Questions

Does it work offline?

Yes. Puzzles are generated on your phone and your progress is saved there. Only Apple's services use a connection: buying or restoring the unlock, and Game Center.

How do I know there's only one solution?

A built-in solver checks every puzzle before you see it. If a board can't be proven to have exactly one solution, it never reaches your screen.

Which devices does it run on?

Any iPhone or iPad running iOS 17 or later. The unlock carries across your devices on the same Apple Account.
