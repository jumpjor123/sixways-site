How it plays

Three ideas, learned in seconds. The depth comes from the honeycomb.

1

Build one chain

Fill every hexagon from 1 to the top number so that consecutive numbers always share a side. Some numbers are printed on the board to start you off.

2

Follow the links

An orange diamond on the border between two hexes means those two cells must hold consecutive numbers. Links are promises — use them to pin the chain down.

3

Trust the puzzle

Every puzzle is generated fresh on your phone and checked by a solver before you see it. If you're stuck, it's your move that's missing — never the puzzle's.

Questions

The short answers. Longer ones live on the support page.

Is it really fully offline?

Yes. Sixways contains no network code — puzzles are generated on your phone and your progress is stored there. The only things that ever use a connection are Apple's own services: buying or restoring the unlock, and posting scores to Game Center. A score earned offline is kept on the device and posted when you're back online.

Is the daily puzzle really the same for everyone?

Yes. The Daily Race puzzle is generated on the phone from the date itself, using a fixed recipe — the same date produces the same board on every device, with no server sending it out. Everyone races the identical puzzle; the fastest time is the headline.

Are there ads or a subscription?

Neither, and there never will be. The only purchase in Sixways is the optional one-time $3.99 unlock for the 60- and 90-cell boards. No ads, no analytics, no tracking, no accounts.

Which iPhones does it run on?

Any iPhone running iOS 17 or later. Puzzles and progress live on the device you play on; the unlock carries across your devices with the same Apple ID — buy once, restore anywhere.

How do I know a puzzle has only one solution?

Every puzzle is checked by a built-in solver before it's shown to you. The solver proves there is exactly one way to complete the chain — if a generated board can't be proven unique, it never reaches your screen.
