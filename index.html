Questions

Does it work offline?

Yes. Puzzles are generated on your device and your progress is saved there. Only the store and the game services use a connection: buying or restoring the unlock, and the leaderboards.

How do I know there's only one solution?

A built-in solver checks every puzzle before you see it. If a board can't be proven to have exactly one solution, it never reaches your screen.

Which devices does it run on?

Any iPhone or iPad running iOS 17 or later, or an Android phone or tablet. The unlock carries across your devices on the same store account — Apple Account or Google account. The free demo also runs in any modern browser.
