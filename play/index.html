How to Play

Build one chain.

Fill every hexagon from 1 to N so consecutive numbers share a side.

Follow the links.

An orange ◆ on a border means those two cells must be consecutive.

Place & edit.

Pick a number below, then tap a cell to place it. Tap a filled cell to select it — then tap a different number to replace it, or tap the cell again to clear it. Fixed numbers can't move.

Difficulty.

Easy and Medium highlight valid moves; Hard and Expert remove the guides.

About Sixways (六道)

Named for the six directions of a honeycomb and inspired by the ClasseTICE number-labyrinth puzzle. Every puzzle is verified to have a single unique solution — generated fresh, or served from a preverified backup when generation runs long.
