Support

Sixways is a hex number-path logic puzzle. Consecutive numbers must sit in touching hexes. Some numbers are printed on the board; an orange diamond between two hexes means those two must hold consecutive numbers. Every puzzle has exactly one solution, proven before you see it.

Questions, problems, or ideas: support@sixways.app

How scoring works

Each board size and difficulty has a par score. Finishing under par time earns a small time bonus (capped at +25% of par). A hint costs 150 points. A placement that conflicts costs 25 points, but only while "Show Mistakes" is on — with it off, mistakes are free and unscored. Undo is always free. Your best score per board size goes to the leaderboard — Game Center on iPhone and iPad, Play Games on Android — if you're signed in; otherwise it's kept on your device.

The unlock

The 36-cell board is the whole game, free, with all four difficulties (Easy, Medium, Hard, Expert) and unlimited puzzles. A single one-time purchase — the Sixways Full Unlock — adds the 60- and 90-cell boards. There are no ads, no subscriptions, and no other purchases.

Restore a purchase: the steps depend on your store.

On iPhone and iPad (App Store): tap "New Puzzle", choose the 60 or 90 size, and tap "Restore Purchase" on the unlock sheet. Make sure you're signed in with the Apple Account that bought it.

On Android (Google Play): tap "New Puzzle", choose the 60 or 90 size, and tap "Restore Purchase" on the unlock sheet. Make sure you're signed in with the Google account that bought it.

On the web: the demo is fully free — there is nothing to buy or restore.

Family sharing: how the unlock is shared depends on your store.

On iPhone and iPad: the unlock is shared with your Apple Family Sharing group. If a family member doesn't see it, ask them to restore the purchase the same way, on their own device.

On Android: Google Play does not share in-app purchases between family members, so the unlock stays with the Google account that bought it and each family member needs their own.

Your devices

Your puzzles and progress live on the device you play on — Sixways does not sync games between devices, or between the app and the web demo. The unlock itself does carry across: buy once, restore on each device signed in to the same store account. If cross-device games matter to you, write and say so; it's the first thing we'd add if players ask for it.

Saved games

An unfinished puzzle is saved automatically and comes back when you reopen the app. A saved game on a board size you no longer have access to (for example after a refund) is kept, not deleted, and becomes playable again if access returns.

If a puzzle is slow to appear

Larger boards, especially 90-cell Expert, can take a few seconds to generate on older devices. If a board ever fails to appear, close and reopen the app — your saved game is kept, and a fresh puzzle will be offered.

Accessibility

Sixways works with your device's screen reader — VoiceOver on iPhone and iPad, TalkBack on Android (every hex describes its number, its neighbours, and whether a placement is legal) — and respects your system text size. If something is hard to read or hear, please write — accessibility reports go to the top of the list.

Privacy

Sixways collects no data: no analytics, no tracking. The full policy is on the privacy page.
